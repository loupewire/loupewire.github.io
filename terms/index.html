1. What Loupewire is

The Extension captures the HTTP traffic the browser makes from every tab in one window, applies header and block rules you define, masks values matching your patterns, and exports the capture as HAR or JSON with masking applied. It observes and edits requests the browser makes on its own; it does not compose requests. It does not capture response bodies, because the browser API it rests on does not provide them and the alternatives would break the privacy promise the Extension makes.
