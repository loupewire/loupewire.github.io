Why Loupewire

Masked before the file exists

Masking at the source

A HAR file is a credential: the tokens inside it are live. Sanitizers clean a file after it exists. Loupewire masks values on screen and in every export before a file exists, so a screenshot and a HAR are both safe to attach without a second pass. A built-in preset covers the usual secret-bearing headers and cookies out of the box.

Every tab in one window

DevTools is per tab and forgets on navigation unless you remember to preserve the log. Flows that cross tabs, such as SSO, OAuth, 3-D Secure and vendor checkout, are exactly the ones you are asked to document. Loupewire records all of them in one tracker window you can leave open all day.

On your device, and only there

Everything runs in the browser. Captured headers, cookies, bodies and URLs stay in the tracker window and are never written to disk unless you export them. The extension makes no network requests of its own. No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no account.

  • Captured traffic lives in the tracker window's memory and is gone when it closes
  • Your patterns, rules and settings stay in the browser's local extension storage
  • Nothing is injected into the pages you visit
  • The privacy policy says the same thing in more words

Honest limits

No response bodies, on purpose

Exports contain request and response headers, cookies, request bodies, timing and redirects. They do not contain response bodies. The browser API this extension uses to observe traffic does not expose them, and Loupewire does not attach a debugger to your tabs to get them. For response bodies, use DevTools alongside Loupewire.

Loupewire also composes no requests and injects nothing into the pages you visit. It observes and edits what the browser sends on its own, and it stands next to DevTools rather than replacing it.

Questions people ask first

The short answers. For anything else, the support page says where to ask.

Is it free?

Yes. Every feature is available today with no account and no sign-up. A paid tier that removes limits on volume and convenience may come later; masking, export and capture stay available on the free tier.

Does it show response bodies?

No. The browser API that makes all-tab capture possible never exposes them. Tools that show bodies attach a debugger to each tab or inject scripts into pages; Loupewire does neither. Use DevTools alongside it for a body.

Does anything leave my machine?

Nothing from the extension. It makes no network requests of its own. This website uses Google Analytics to count visits; the extension does not, and the privacy policy keeps the two apart.

Which browsers?

Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers.

How is it different from DevTools?

DevTools shows one tab and shows raw tokens. Loupewire shows every tab in one window and masks tokens before you see them. Use both: DevTools for response bodies and the console, Loupewire for the evidence you attach.

Can I use it on a company laptop?

That is what it is for. It needs no system proxy, no root certificate and no admin rights, only the extension permissions the store shows you. Follow your employer's policy on what you attach to tickets.

What does "masked" mean, exactly?

A masked value shows its first and last characters and hides the rest, on screen and in every export. Masking is a display transform; the extension keeps no copy of the value anywhere.

Where do I report a bug or ask a question?

In the public loupewire-support repository on GitHub. Attach a masked export, say which browser and version, and describe what you expected.
